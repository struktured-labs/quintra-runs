Issue #228 — did #221 raise contact deaths?

quintra#228. Perf pass #221 may have raised contact deaths — isolated with 3a910dbe

  • Midpoint 3a910dbe (PR #221 only) was run with the same Player method as the 684bac5c baseline and the 805476ea re-baseline: Cinder Rex balance_arena.py (5 heroes × 3 skills × 4 seeds, Easy + Normal) and stage-3 balance_rooms.py (80 fixtures × 3 skills × 2 seeds = 480 fights each). Room fixtures matched 80/80 names and enemy_kinds across all three builds, so per-subset contact deltas are comparable. d221 = 3a910dbe − 684bac5c (perf pass, same Burn/DoT rules). d227 = 805476ea − 3a910dbe (Burn trim + lethal Poison/Bleed, same perf).
  • Contact-only Easy (480 fights): 56 → 40 → 54. The perf pass lowered Easy contact-only by 16. #227 then raised it by 14, back near the baseline. Maw/Kite contact 35 → 28 → 42 (d221 −7, d227 +14). Rooms with no kind 11/14/25: 13 → 8 → 8.
  • Contact-only Normal (480 fights): 41 → 56 → 98. The perf pass raised Normal contact-only by 15, almost entirely in rooms with no Maw, Kite, or Fold Star (18 → 31 → 32; d221 +13, d227 +1). Maw/Kite contact only went 22 → 25 → 66 (d221 +3, d227 +41). Fold Star contact 9 → 12 → 25 (d221 +3, d227 +13).
  • The large Normal contact-only spike versus the 684 baseline is #227, not #221: after the Maw/Kite Burn proc trim, Burn-involved deaths fell 181 → 102 and contact-only jumped 56 → 98. Five lethal Poison/Bleed (dot_kill) deaths appear only on 805476ea.
  • Overall Normal deaths did rise on #221: 251 → 296 → 264 (d221 +45, d227 −32). Maw/Kite died/N 199/288 (69%) → 226/288 (78%) → 193/288 (67%). Of the +27 extra Maw/Kite deaths on the perf pass, +23 were Burn-involved and only +3 were contact-only. That does not reproduce the earlier 180-fight average-skill observation that the extra deaths were mostly contact, at least not under this 480-fight Player method.
  • Cinder Rex contact is unchanged across all three builds: Easy 21/22/21 clears and 39/38/39 deaths (all contact-only); Normal 0 clears and 60/60/60 deaths (all contact-only). Entry foyer was mixed (Easy 7 → 11 → 12, Normal 41 → 35 → 36) and is not a curated Burn/Stop room.
  • Verdict: #221 clearly raised Normal stage-3 lethality (especially Maw/Kite, and mostly as Burn-involved deaths) and modestly raised Normal contact-only in rooms that have no Burn/Stop carriers. It did not clearly raise Maw/Kite contact deaths, and Easy contact-only fell. The big contact-only jump vs 684bac5c is #227. #228 stays open as a lethality / perf question; the “extra deaths are mostly contact” mechanism is not supported by this Player sweep.
